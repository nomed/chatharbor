Desktop-first prototype. A synthetic archive preview is in development; phone acquisition and encrypted recovery are not yet available. No software can guarantee automatic extraction of all WhatsApp data from an iPhone or restoration of memberships after an account ban.
Core capabilities

Preserve more than messages.

Backups help with devices. Continuity also means retaining context, important relationships and a plan for rebuilding access.

01 / ARCHIVE

Conversation archive

Keep original chat exports and available media, with formats you can read independently of the original messaging app.

02 / CONNECTIONS

Group recovery registry

Record communities, group roles, administrator contacts, invitation methods and rejoin instructions.

03 / CONFIDENCE

Recovery readiness

Identify missing contacts, unverified invitations and gaps in archived conversations before an emergency.

04 / CONTROL

Portable, private records

Local-first data, verifiable export manifests and independently recoverable encrypted copies controlled by the user.

Planned approach

Desktop-first. User-controlled.

1. Acquire

Qualify authorized local iPhone and Android backup acquisition on desktop. Manual chat exports remain a fallback.

2. Organize

Associate archived snapshots with conversations and recovery information.

3. Recover

Follow documented steps to contact administrators and request rejoining with a new number.

Transparent boundaries

No impossible promises.

iOS does not provide a supported interface for continuously reading the complete WhatsApp database. Chat exports may be truncated, do not cover all account metadata, and cannot automatically restore history or group membership. Rejoining requires the platform's normal access rules and any necessary administrator approval.

ChatHarbor is independent of WhatsApp and Meta. Its public documentation website never asks for, processes or stores user messages.
