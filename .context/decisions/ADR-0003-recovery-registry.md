# ADR-0003 — Recovery registry is not membership restoration

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-07

## Context
After a ban, users may lose group memberships and administrator access.

## Decision proposed
Maintain an independent, manually verified registry of group/community names, user roles, contact channels and invitation paths. Rejoining is an external, user-mediated process subject to group and platform rules.

## Consequences
The registry requires periodic user maintenance. Stored invite links are sensitive. No feature claims programmatic recovery of a WhatsApp membership.
